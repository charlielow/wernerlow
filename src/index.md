# About Werner

Werner Low (September 7, 1947 – November 14, 2015) was a **writer** and **creative director** based in Boston.

**Literary work.** His short stories and poems appeared in a couple dozen literary magazines. A few are collected here, in [Writing](/writing/), along with excerpts from two completed novels, *Don’t Worry, Dandelo* and *The Prophet of Essaouira*.

**Commercial work.** Werner’s work included award-winning video and multimedia presentations, messaging and speech writing for senior executives at global corporations, and concepts for marketing collateral and web-based communications. [More about his commercial work](/commercial-work-page/).
