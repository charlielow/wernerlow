About Werner Low

Werner Low's stories and poems have appeared (or are scheduled to appear) in Falling Fountain, The Journal (of Ohio State University), Piedmont Literary Review, Pinehurst Journal, The Literary Review (of Trinity College, Hartford), and The Square Table (January, 2007). He lives in Cambridge, Massachusetts with no caged pets.
Any reproduction or distribution of the content on WernerLow.com without written consent from the author is strictly forbidden.
