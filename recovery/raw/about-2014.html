About Werner

Werner Low is a writer and creative director based in Boston.

Literary Work:  His short stories and poems have appeared in a couple dozen literary magazines. A few are available here, in “Literary Work.”  A new novel, Don’t Worry, Dandelo, is looking for a publisher.

Commercial Work:  Werner’s work includes award-winning video and multimedia presentations, messaging and speech writing for senior executives at global corporations, and concepts for marketing collateral and web-based communications.
