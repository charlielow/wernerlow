Commercial Work

During his tenure as Creative Director at Envision, a nationally recognized media production company, Werner’s video and multimedia work won numerous awards, including a Grand Award at the New York Festival, and awards at AMI and AMI International, as well as at Intercom and the ITVA festivals.

He has served as speechwriter and messaging maven to top executives at some of the world’s leading companies, including Converse, Fidelity, Foot-Joy, Gillette, IBM, Lotus, Siemens, OSRAM/Sylvania, State Street and Titleist.

He’s also developed concepts and copy for marketing collateral and websites for a broad range of companies, with a particular focus on financial service organizations.
