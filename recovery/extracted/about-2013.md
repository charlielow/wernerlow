Werner Low is a **writer** and **creative director** based in Boston and New York.

**Literary Work**:  His short stories and poems have appeared in more than a dozen literary magazines. Some are available here, in “Literary Work.”  A new novel, *Don’t Worry, Dandelo*, is looking for a publisher.

**Commercial Work**:  Werner’s work includes award-winning video and multimedia presentations, messaging and speech writing for senior executives at global corporations, and concepts for marketing collateral and web-based communications.
